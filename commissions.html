Custom Commissions

Every piece on this site started as a blank page and a feeling. If you want something made just for you — art, branding, or a whole online presence — this is where it starts.

Something else in mind? If you can describe it, ask — if I can’t do it or learn to, I will find someone else who can.

How it works

  1. You reach out Tell me what you’re dreaming of — the piece, the feeling, the size you’re picturing.
  2. We talk it through Style, scope, timeline, and price — worked out together, no surprises.
  3. I build it Dot by dot, line by line — with progress updates along the way so it’s yours at every step.
  4. It’s yours The finished original, made for no one else.
