Custom Commissions

Every piece on this site started as a blank page and a feeling. If you want something made just for you — art, branding, or a whole online presence — this is where it starts.

What I can make for you

  • Pointillism artwork — portraits and pieces built dot by deliberate dot, the patient way. No realism yet — stylized all the way.
  • Abstract & standard artwork — marker, ink, pastel, or acrylic, straight out of the sketchbook.
  • Album art — digital or hand-drawn, made to match your sound.
  • Logos — for bands, artists, and businesses.
  • T-shirt graphics — designs ready for print.
  • Business cards & print — cards, flyers, and promo pieces.
  • Gig posters & event flyers — loud, eye-catching promo for your next show or event.
  • Social media branding — banners, profile art, and post templates with one consistent look.
  • EPKs — electronic press kits that make you look like the headliner you are.
  • Custom websites — your own corner of the internet, designed around your brand from the ground up.

Something else in mind? If you can describe it, ask — if I can’t do it or learn to, I will find someone else who can.

How it works

  1. You reach out Tell me what you’re dreaming of — the piece, the feeling, the size you’re picturing.
  2. We talk it through Style, scope, timeline, and price — worked out together, no surprises.
  3. I build it Dot by dot, line by line — with progress updates along the way so it’s yours at every step.
  4. It’s yours The finished original, made for no one else.
