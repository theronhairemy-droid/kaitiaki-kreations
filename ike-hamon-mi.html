Ike (Isaac) Hamon

He wāhanga o te whakaaturanga whānau o Hamon Family Entertainment. Nā Ike anō āna mahi: kei te ringatoi ngā motika me te mana.

Isaac “Ike” Hamon he kaiwhakatere me te kairuruku mahi tū e noho ana i Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand. He poto ake tana ake haurongo Instagram: “director, stunt coordinator, show-off”.

E tautoko ana te mahi i tēnā. Ko Ike te kairuruku mahi tū i M3GAN 2.0, e ārahi ana i tētahi rōpū neke atu i te whitu tekau ngā kaiwhakaari me ngā kairig, ā, i ruruku mahi tū ia, i whakaari anō hoki i ngā mahi mea ora me te hopu nekehanga me Wētā FX i Resident Evil a Zach Cregger. Kei roto i āna mahi mata ko Zack Snyder’s Justice League (2021), Mortal Engines (2018), me War for the Planet of the Apes (2017), ā, kua kawea ia e āna mahi nekehanga ki ngā wāhi tawhiti pērā i Inia.

E tū ana ia i roto i te Expansion: te whakatupuranga i kawe i te korakora Hamon ki te ao, ki ētahi o ngā huinga kiriata nui rawa o te ao.
