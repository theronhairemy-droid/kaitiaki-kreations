Hamon Family Entertainment: Te Kaitiaki

Albert Awanui Hamon

He wāhanga o te whakaaturanga whānau o Hamon Family Entertainment. Nā Albert anō āna toi: kei te ringatoi ngā motika me te mana.

Albert Awanui Hamon te tama a Rei Hamon, ā, ko ia te kaitiaki o ngā toi o te whānau. Ka ārahi tana taiwhanga ki te rārangi i waiho e tōna pāpā ki a ia:

“Ko tāku i ako mai i tōku pāpā a Rei, ehara i te ako noa i ngā toi pai, engari he aroha ki ngā momo ora katoa me tō tātou hononga ki te ao whānui.”

He ringatoi mahi maha ia i te tino tikanga: te peita i te tūturutanga me te moemoeā, te whakairo rākau, wheua me te pounamu, me te peita i ngā pakitara nui. I whānau mai i te Thames Coast o te Coromandel Peninsula, Ngāti Porou, i whakangungua ia mā tētahi akoranga Commercial Art me te Australian Art Training Institute i Melbourne, ā, kua kawea e ia te mahi puta noa i Aotearoa, i Ahitereiria, i Āhia, i Amerika ki te Tonga tae atu ki te Amazon, me ngā whenua tekau mā tahi o te USA. I tonoa ia e te Kāwanatanga o Aotearoa ki te hanga i tana mahi mahi maha nui Aoraki Family, i te rākau, te angaanga me te peita, mō te whare o te Social Welfare Department i Timaru, ā, ka huri tana raupapa o ināianei ki ngā manu o Ahitereiria, tae atu ki ngā kāhu korokoro.

Hōhonu ngā tau whakaako. I whakaako a Albert i te toi, te whakairo wheua me ngā uku mō te Aoraki Youth Trust, i āwhina ki te hoahoa anō i ngā wāhanga hītori māori, Māori me ngā Pākehā tōmua o ngā whare taonga o Motueka me Timaru, ā, i whakaako i te whakairo wheua i Thames High School me Matai Whitu Marae i Kopu. I Rotorua Boys’ High School i pau i a ia ngā tau e whitu hei Artist in Residence, i hoahoa i ngā akoranga toi Māori i tangohia e ngā kura tuarua puta noa i te motu, ā, i mutu hei Tumuaki o te Tari Toi. I pōtitia anō ia hei māngai mō Kai Tahu me te Royal Forest and Bird Society i ngā tūtohutanga ki te kāwanatanga mō te hanga papa moana.

I roto i ēnei katoa ko ia te pou, te pūmau, o te Rei Hamon Gallery me Hamon Art NZ, e whakarite ana i ngā whakaaturanga o ngā mahi a tōna pāpā me āna ake puta noa i Aotearoa me Ahitereiria. I tāpae tahi a pāpā rāua ko tama i ngā mahi taketake me ngā tānga iti ki ngā kaupapa aroha tiaki taiao mō ngā tau, e kohi pūtea ana hei tiaki i ngā manu mōrea, inarā te kōkako me te tūturiwhatu o Aotearoa. He kaituhi whakaputa anō a Albert: te raupapa Ozzma and Kauai, The Jade Hook me The Forbidden Wave. Ko ia te take e ora tonu ai tēnei whakapapa ki tētahi kāinga, ā, ko ia te take i tipu ake ai tētahi tamaiti i Edmonton e mōhio pū ana nō wai ōna ira.
