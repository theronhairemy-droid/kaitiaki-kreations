Ngā Ota Motuhake

I tīmata ia mahi o tēnei pae hei whārangi pātea me tētahi kare ā-roto. Ki te hiahia koe i tētahi mea i hangaia mōu anake, te toi, te waitohu, tētahi aroaro tuihono katoa rānei, koinei te tīmatanga.

Ngā mea ka taea e au te hanga māu

Pāwhiria tētahi ratonga hei whakauru ki te puka tono i raro.

  • Ngā whakaahua me ngā mahi i hangaia, ira kōtahi āta whakaaro i ia wā, te ara manawanui. Kāore anō he tino rite, he āhua auaha katoa.

  • Te pene rongo, te waituhi, te pastel, te acrylic rānei, tika mai i te pukapuka huahua.

  • He matihiko, he mea tā ā-ringa rānei, i hangaia kia hāngai ki tō tangi.

  • Mō ngā pēne, ngā ringatoi, me ngā pakihi.

  • Ngā hoahoa kua rite mō te tā.

  • Ngā kāri, ngā pānui, me ngā mahi whakatairanga.

  • He whakatairanga kaha, hopu kanohi mō tō whakaaturanga, tō hui e whai ake.

  • Ngā kara, te toi kōtaha, me ngā tauira whakaputa me te āhua kotahi e rite ana.

  • Ngā kete pāpāho hiko e whakaatu ana i a koe hei kaiwhakaari matua, pērā i a koe tonu.

  • Tō ake kokonga o te ipurangi, i hoahoatia i runga i tō waitohu mai i te tūāpapa.

He mea kē atu i tō hinengaro? Ki te taea e koe te whakaahua, pātai mai, ki te kore e taea e au, e kore rānei e taea te ako, ka kimi au i tētahi atu ka taea.

Te āhua o te mahi

  1. Ka whakapā mai koe Kōrero mai i tāu e moemoeā ana, te mahi, te kare ā-roto, te rahi e whakaarohia ana e koe.
  2. Ka kōrero tahi tāua Te āhua, te whānuitanga, te wātaka, me te utu, i whiriwhiria tahitia, kāore he ohorere.
  3. Ka hangaia e au Ira kōtahi, rārangi kōtahi i ia wā, me ngā whakamōhiotanga ahunga i te ara, kia nōu i ia kaupae.
  4. Nōu taua mahi Te mahi taketake kua oti, i hangaia mō tētahi atu kore.

Tīmatahia tētahi ota

Ka tīmata ngā ota i tētahi kōrero. Tukua mai he karere tūmataiti i Instagram, ā, kōrero mai i tāu e moemoeā ana, whakakīia rānei te puka i raro, ā, ka tau tika ki taku pouakarere.

  • Instagram, @hairemyverse

Tonoa tētahi ota

Kua tae mai te tono. Ka pānui au, ā, ka whakapā atu ki a koe ki te kōrero tahi.
