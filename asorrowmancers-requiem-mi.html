A Sorrowmancer’s Requiem

A Sorrowmancer’s Requiem he kōrero roa pakiwaitara pōuri, e ono ngā pukapuka, kei Vornhaven te wāhi, he ao i hangaia i te tukinga o ngā aorangi tawhito e rua, o Synxxor me Deviorre, he ao e raranga ana te pōuri ki roto i te oneone, ā, ko te mamae tonu he makutu.

I te wā i tīhaea ai a Reia Shadowmark, te wahine tuku iho o te hapū Sorrowmancer o Veilmourne, i tōna moenga i te pō e te rangatira whawhai Serpimancer, e Vexis Darktail, ka whakakotahi tōna pāpā, a Lorien, te kaikawe o te Havenblade, i ngā morehu kua pakaru hei ope whawhai, ā, ka whai ia i a Vexis puta noa i ngā whenua o ngā hapū tauwhāinga: te Shadow Path kua kanga, te moutere whakawarea o Bryymsland, te tāone kūaha mura o Ashendrel, me tua atu. E whakapono ana a Vexis he ngoikore te pōuri. Ko te whakautu a Lorien ko te karanga whawhai o te kōrero katoa: ehara tō mātou pōuri i te ngoikore, ko tō mātou kaha kē.

Puta noa i ngā pukapuka e ono, ka kohia e te ope whawhai ngā maramara tiretire kua marara o ngā hapū, he kī ia mea ki ngā mana tawhito ake i ngā torona e kerēme ana i a rātou, mai i ngā whakamātautau waiata raranga o ngā Vitaweaver huna, ki Xes'zʧorath, te Maramara Venom e rāhuitia ana, e whakapōuri ana tōna ingoa tonu i te arero o te tangata o waho e māia ana ki te kōrero. Engari ka tapahi whakaroto anō te rapunga, pērā i te tapahi whakawaho: mā ngā hīkoi wairua thriffinberry i te ao o ngā atua, mā te pōuri, te paihana, me te atarangi e kōhimuhimu ana, me aro ia tangata ki ngā mea e whakamataku ana i a rātou i mua i tō rātou aro ki ngā mea e whai ana i a rātou. Ka hui katoa ki Paradox Isles, i reira ka tīhaea te rangi e ngā maramara kua whakakotahi, ā, ka haere mai ngā mana katoa o Vornhaven ki te whawhai mō te mea ka ara ake i ngā pungarehu.

He kōrero mō te pōuri kua huri hei mana, mō te utu me te tohu, me tētahi whānau e kore e tūturi.

Ngā Pukapuka

  1. The Key That Unlocks Darkness Ka tangohia a Reia; ka hanga te ope whawhai, ā, ka tīmata te whai, te rapu i te maramara Noctomancer.
  2. To Bid You Farewell Ka pakaru te ope whawhai i te pōuri; he tangihanga, he rapunga kite, me tētahi kawenata huna, te murunga hara mō te maramara Ursmite.
  3. Tonight In Flames Te Whakamātautau ahi i Ashendrel; te mate, te aranga, me tētahi hononga uaua, ngā Trials mō te maramara Pyrosire.
  4. Sonorous Harmonious Ngā whakamātautau waiata raranga o ngā Vitaweaver huna; ka whāki te aroha, me ngā himene e oro ana ki te maramara Vitaweaver.
  5. Xes'zʧorath Te Huaki o Jagged’s End; te raweke o te maramara Serpimancer i te puku o te āwhā.
  6. Paradox Isles Te whawhai i Paradox Isles: i hui ngā mana katoa i tētahi whawhai nui mō te āmua o Vornhaven.
