Te Kōrero mō MANAIA

Te kōrero kua whakaritea

I roto i te toi Māori, he kaitiaki te manaia, he karere i waenganui i ngā ao: he wāhi manu, he wāhi nakahi, he wāhi mea tawhito ake i ngā mea e rua. Ka kawe ōna koru i te tupunga hou, ā, ka kōrero ngā here raranga mō te whakapapa, ngā rārangi e here ana i ngā whakatupuranga.

I ngā ringa o Kaitiaki

He kaitiaki te MANAIA a Kaitiaki i tāia mō te tangata kotahi: a Sammy Bright, tōna punga. I makā te MANAIA tuatahi e Jimmy. Ko te mea tuarua, he toi ira pene i runga papanga 18 ki te 24 īnihi, i hipa i te 110 hāora i te 7 o Noema, 2026, ā, ina mutu, ka hoatu ki a Sammy.
