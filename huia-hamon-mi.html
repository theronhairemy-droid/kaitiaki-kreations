Huia Hamon

He wāhanga o te whakaaturanga whānau o Hamon Family Entertainment. Nā Huia anō āna puoro: kei te ringatoi ngā motika me te mana.

Huia Hamon, te tamāhine a Awanui me te mokopuna a Rei, he kaihanga puoro me te ringatoi toa e kawe ana i te whakapapa ki roto i te puoro Māori o te anamata. Ko tana waiata “Waka Huia” te Kiwi Track o’ the Day i taku whakaputanga rangitaki tino pai. Tōna haurongo, i āna ake kupu:

Me ngā pao hiko, te patu ora, ngā kitā ora me ngā oro hōhonu, he oro nui paparanga whai rawa te oro a Huia me ngā kupu whai whakaaro i te reo Māori me ngā pā o te reo Pākehā me te reo Pāniora.

Hei kaihanga puoro toa, me te ringatoi ‘Best Wahine Māori’ i te tau 2022, ka rere āna puoro mai i te DnB wai āmio me Baitercell me Rei, ki ngā waiata dancehall kaha, ā, he āhua oraora, he hoa hoki tōna i te atamira. He pai anō ki a Huia ngā huihuinga iti, tata, e māmā ana me tētahi kitā me tōna reo anake.

Kua whakaari ia i te nuinga o ngā taiopenga nui rawa o Aotearoa pērā i Splore me RnV, me ngā wāhi o te ao i Potukara me Kānata. E mōhiotia ana āna puoro he puoro taketake Māori o te anamata, ā, e kawe ana i ngā karere tuku iho mō te manaaki me te kotahitanga (unity).
