Timi Rodgers

He wāhanga o te whakaaturanga whānau o Hamon Family Entertainment. Nā Timi anō āna puoro: kei te ringatoi ngā motika me te mana.

Timi Rodgers tōku teina. I Vancouver e hanga puoro ana, e ārahi ana hoki ia i Nocturnal Angel: doom, grunge, me prog. Ko tā rātou waiata tuarua “Fantasy” te Canadian Track of the Day i Rangitaki #2. Te haurongo o te pēne, i ā rātou ake kupu:

Ko Nocturnal Angel he pēne i awea e te Doom, te Grunge, me te Prog e noho ana i Vancouver, Canada, he putanga auaha mō Timi Rodgers (kaihanga puoro/kaiwaiata) i muri i tōna hīkoi atu mō tētahi wā mai i tana pēne tuatahi i te tāone, a Green Alderson. I muri i te tuhi i āna waiata tuatahi ruarua, i uaua ki a Timi te kimi i ngā mema pēne pūmau mō te kaupapa, tae noa ki te kuhu mai o ana hoa me ana hoa noho nā te aroha ki te puoro, me te hiahia ki te āwhina i a ia ki te huri i āna titonga waiata hei huinga oraora.

Ka mihi tuatahi rātou ki a koe me ngā oro waiata mārū, whakamarie, kātahi ka whai ko ngā riff mura me ngā waiata kitā whakawiri wairua. Me ngā kupu e hāngai ana, e whakaaroaro hōhonu ana hoki, kei te whakarite a Nocturnal Angel ki te ruku hōhonu ake ki ō rātou pakiaka i te puoro progressive, grunge, me doom, me te whāinga kōtahi: ki te whakakotahi i te pouri mai i tētahi taha o tā rātou papanga puoro, me te ataahua me te mātauranga o tētahi atu.
