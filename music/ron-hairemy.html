Ron Hairemy

Ron Hairemy is the evolution of a sound, a musical identity born from necessity. While still a deeply emotional space, this era shifts the focus firmly toward personal growth and systematic defiance. Leaving the past behind, it is defined by rebellion and unapologetic self-reclamation. The music strips away the older emotional weight to deliver a more grounded energy, built on hard-won lessons and the mechanics of emotional survival. Craigles O’Grady had to step aside so this voice could become fully realized.

The Death of Craigles is the album that buries the past, growth, rebellion, and self-reclamation. And the debut acapella single “Momentarily” is in the works: a song about loving yourself so abundantly it pours into empty cups, instead of setting yourself on fire to keep someone else warm.
