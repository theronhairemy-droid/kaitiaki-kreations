Kia ora, hello, and welcome in

I’m Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon, born Craig Rodgers, and I record as Ron Hairemy. I make pointillism the patient way, dot by deliberate dot, the way my great-uncle Rei Hamon saw the bush: no shortcuts, just time turned into image. I make music the same way, hip hop, heart, and hard-won lessons, pressed into verses.

This is the home for all of it: the galleries, the songs, the world of Vornhaven, and the morning blog. Have a look around, stay for a track, and if something here moves you, you’re in the right place.

Kia ora, ya hoser!, pull up a chair.
