About the artist

Craig draws pointillism — thousands of dots, one patient piece at a time. He performs and records music as Ron Hairemy, carves Māori whakairo, and is writing A Sorrowmancer’s Requiem. Every drawing is catharsis through addiction and mental health recovery: the work is the way through.

The whakapapa thread runs deep. His great-granduncle Francis Rei Paul Hamon (1919–2008) was the renowned Māori pointillist of the New Zealand bush — and his work is the root of Craig’s own pointillism practice, carried forward dot by dot.
