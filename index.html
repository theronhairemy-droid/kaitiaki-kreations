Kia ora, hello, and welcome in

I’m Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon, and I record as Ron Hairemy. I make pointillism the patient way, dot by deliberate dot, the way my great-uncle Rei Hamon saw the bush: no shortcuts, just time turned into image. I make music the same way, hip hop, heart, and hard-won lessons, pressed into verses.

This is the home for all of it: the galleries, the songs, the world of Vornhaven, and the morning blog. Have a look around, stay for a track, and if something here moves you, you’re in the right place.

Kia ora, ya hoser!, pull up a chair.

Latest finished pointillism

anxieTREE 3.0

Pointillism, 18×24″ canvas · 2026

The full realization, the third of three ~125-hour anxieTREE versions: the eye-hollowed tree, the beam, the cratered moon. Drawn against the anxiety, finished for the room. This canvas sold at a silent auction at Cristopher Ryan (Oddiologist)’s memorial show, helping his family cover funeral costs.

On the canvas now: the 18×24″ MANAIA redo, 35+ hours in, linework done, colour next.
