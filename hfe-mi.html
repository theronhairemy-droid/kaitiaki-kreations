Whakangahau ā-Whānau Hamon

Ngā kaihanga o te whānau Hamon, te toi me te puoro i roto i te toto, mai i te ngahere o Aotearoa ki Edmonton.

Ngā Mana o te Whānau

Ehara ngā mahi toi, ngā puoro, me ngā mahi tahi e whakaaturia ana i tēnei whārangi i ahau, nō tōku whānau kē. Kei tōku whānau ngā mana me te rangatiratanga o ngā mea katoa nō rātou e whakaaturia ana, e honoa ana rānei i konei. Kua kohia me te aroha, hei whakahonore i te rārangi i ahu mai ai, kāore tētahi mea o tēnei whārangi e whakawhiti i tētahi mana ki tētahi atu, ki ahau anō hoki.

He rārangi kotahi, he ringa maha

Kāore te toi i tīmata i ahau, ā, kāore e mutu i ahau. I ahu mai au i tētahi rārangi kaihanga: tētahi matua keke nui i pau tōna oranga ki te tā i te ngahere o Aotearoa, ira kōtahi i ia wā, tāna tama e pupuri ora ana i taua tuku ihotanga i ngā taiwhanga i tēnei rā, ā, tētahi whakatupuranga o ngā whanaunga e peita ana, e whakairo ana, e hanga puoro ana i tērā taha o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa. Ko taku ake toi ira, ia ira, i ahu mai i tēnei rārangi. He tohu ara tēnei whārangi ki te whānau: whaia ngā hononga, ā, tirohia te mahi i te wāhi e noho ana. Kua tuhia katoatia te rārangi i taku whakapapa.

Timi Rodgers

© Timi Rodgers / Nocturnal Angel, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

Taku teina, ā, te reo o Nocturnal Angel, te doom, te grunge, me te prog mai i Vancouver. Te kaiwhakaputa me te kaiwaiata o te pēne: ngā rangi ngāwari, mārire i te tuatahi, kātahi ka tau ngā riff kaha. Ko tā rātou waiata tuarua, ko “Fantasy”, te Waiata Kānata o te Rā i Rangitaki #2.

Ike (Isaac) Hamon

© Ike Hamon, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

E tū ana a Ike i Te Horanga, te whakatupuranga i kawe i te korakora Hamon ki te ao, mā ngā mahi tuku ihotanga me te whakaputa. E haere ana te ingoa nā te mea nā tēnei whakatupuranga i kawe.

Nate Hamon

© Nate Hamon / Mayan Fox, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

Te taringa Hamon, kua huri ki te tangi. E rere ana te puoro a Nate i raro i te ingoa Mayan Fox, he mea āhuarangi, whānui te toro, kua whakawhitia ngā ira mō ngā ngaru. Kua oti kē tāna “Mighty Wonders” te whakaatu i ngā kōwhiringa LAWNgevity a Kaitiaki.

Francis Hamon

© Francis Hamon, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

E mau ana ia i te ingoa katoa o tōna tupuna, a Francis Rei Paul Hamon. He kaipeita, he kaitā pakitara e mahi ana i ngā kaupapa Māori, te tiki, te koru, te manaia, i runga i te papanga me te peita rehu, ā, he kaiwhakataetae i te whakataetae Art Battle o Auckland. He whanaunga tuarua nō Kaitiaki; nō te tau 2026 anake i kitea ai rāua e tētahi, e tētahi, mā tēnei pae tonu.

Huia Hamon

© Huia Hamon, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

Te tamāhine a Awanui, te mokopuna wahine a Rei, ā, he kaiwaiata. Ko te waiata a Huia, ko “Waka Huia”, te Waiata Kiwi o te Rā a Kaitiaki i tana whakaputanga rangitaki tino pai: te reo o tētahi whanaunga e kawe ana i te kāinga i runga i Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa.

Albert Awanui Hamon

© Albert Awanui Hamon, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te ringatoi.

Te tama a Rei, ā, te kaitiaki o te rārangi. He ringatoi mahi maha e mahi ana, te tino rite, te surrealism, te whakairo, ngā tānga pakitara, ā, te tangata i muri i te Rei Hamon Gallery me Hamon Art NZ, e kawe ana i ngā whakaaturanga o tōna pāpā i Aotearoa me Ahitereiria. I te reo, ko Arapata tōna ingoa: te ingoa o te tupuna, e mau ana i tōna āhua Ingarihi.

Rei Hamon

© Te whānau o Rei Hamon, ko ngā mana me te rangatiratanga kei te whānau.

Francis Rei Paul Hamon (1919–2008). Te pūtake o ngā mea katoa o tēnei whārangi: te ringatoi toi ira Māori rongonui i pau tōna oranga ki te tā i te ngahere o Aotearoa, ira kōtahi i ia wā. Te tupuna keke o Kaitiaki, ā, te take e hanga ai te peka o Edmonton o tēnei whānau i āna toi, ira kōtahi i ia wā.
