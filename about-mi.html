I Muri i te Catharsis

Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon, i whānau mai ko Craig Rodgers, i puta ko Craigles O’Grady, ā, i hangaia anō ko Ron Hairemy, he ringatoi e hora ana āna mahi ki ngā mahi maha. Ka tāia e ia te toi ira, miriona ira i ia wā, ka whakaari, ka hopu puoro, ā, kei te tuhi ia i te raupapa pukapuka A Sorrowmancer’s Requiem.

E herea ana tana hononga ki tēnei mahi ki tōna tuku ihotanga, e mau ana i te ahi auaha o tōna matua keke, o te ringatoi Māori rongonui a Francis Rei Paul Hamon, ā, e tiki ana hoki i te whakaaweawe hōhonu mai i te tirohanga toi a Albert Awanui Hamon. Ehara tēnei tuhinga āta o te ao mā te toi ira i te kōrero o mua noa iho; he mahere ora ia e kawea whakamua ana, e huri ana i te wāhi pātea hei ao uaua, ira kōtahi āta whakaaro i ia wā.

Mā te auaha e ora ai ia. Ahakoa te tā, te tuhi, te hanga puoro rānei, he mahi āta whakaaro ia mahi mō te catharsis, e whakapūmau ana i a ia ki tētahi kaupapa kei tua atu i te papanga, ā, e tuhi ana i tētahi mahere pūmau i roto i te whakaoranga o te hauora hinengaro.

Te Whakapapa o Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon

Te Kaihokohoko Matua
John Peter Pruden (1778–1868), te kaihokohoko matua o Hudson’s Bay Company, ā, i muri mai he chief factor, ā, he kaikaunihera o Assiniboia, te pūtake o te rārangi Pruden. He wahine Cree tana wahine tuatahi, ā, nā tōna oranga i ngā ara tauhokohoko o te raki i here tēnei whānau ki ngā iwi me ngā awa o ngā mānia i mua noa atu i te noho o tētahi Kānata hei tapanga mō rātou. Nā Pruden te ingoa i hoatu ki te teihana tauhokohoko i te North Saskatchewan: ko Edmonton, i muri i te tāone i Middlesex, Ingarangi, i whānau mai ai ia. E mau ana te tāone e tū ana ki reira i tēnei rā i tāna kupu.
Te Puna o te Kite
Arapata Taniwha (c.1820–1888), te Matakite rongonui, nā tōna tirohanga wairua me tōna hononga hōhonu ki te ao kāore e kitea i puta ai ngā korakora tuatahi o te māramatanga mō tō tātou whānau.
Te Arawhata o ngā Whakatupuranga
Henare (Henry) Hamon, te hononga nui i kawe i te tuku ihotanga o Taniwha ki roto i te rārangi Hamon, te pāpā o Rei, ā, te tupuna matua o tēnei whakatupuranga o nāianei.
Te Pūtake
Henry Hixon Hamon, te tūāpapa, nā tōna aroha hōhonu, tūāpapa ki te taiao me te kōrero paki i whakaaweawe tētahi tuku ihotanga o ngā kaihanga e kite ana i te ao mā te tirohanga toi.
Ngā Pou
Trevor Clarke Hamon & Rei Hamon, ngā tohunga o te mahi. Mai i te kaha pūmau o Trevor ki te toi ira rongonui a Rei, “ira kōtahi i ia wā”.
Te Horanga
Lorena Hamon Mowser, Albert Awanui Hamon, Nate Hamon, Ike (Isaac) Hamon, & Huia Hamon, te whakatupuranga i kawe i te korakora ki te ao whānui. Ko Albert, te tama a Rei, i kawe whakamua i te rārangi hei pāpā mō Nate rāua ko Huia. Mai i te whakaora i Middle Earth i Weta Workshop ki ngā tangi āhuarangi o Mayan Fox, ā, ki ngā mahi nui i te tuku ihotanga me te whakaputa.
Te Tuku Ihotanga Ora
Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon, e kawe ana i te rama i roto i te uaua o te mahi, te manawataki o te puoro, ā, ngā one hurihuri o te ao Terrifor, me te ao o Vornhaven.
Ngā Kaitiaki o te Ingoa
Matthew Taniwha, Trevor Eric, Carlee Terina-Ane, me Timothy Joseph, ngā teina-tuākana e kawe ana i te taumahatanga me te mana o ngā tūpuna i roto i ō rātou ingoa tonu, e pupuri ora ana i ngā kōrero o Taniwha me Hamon.
Te Punga
Sammy, te hoa pūmau, te kaha whakapūmau, e tohatoha ana i te manawanui o te mahi me te hanga o tēnei tuku ihotanga.
Te Āmuri
Xander & Leia-Lorena, te whakatupuranga e whai ake, e tohu ana kāore anō kia mutu te kōrero o tō tātou whānau, me tōna ahi auaha.

Te Tūāpapa o te Haerenga

Ehara tēnei whakapapa i te rārangi ingoa noa; ko te manawa ia o ia kupu ka tuhia, o ia ao ka hangaia. Mai i te tirohanga wairua o Arapata Taniwha ki te manawanui āta o Rei Hamon, he tuku ihotanga ora tēnei kōrero e tukua iho ana i roto i te toto. Ehara tēnei mahi mō te mahi noa iho; kei konei hei whakahonore i te ahi whakapuaki i tahu a Henry Hixon Hamon, he ahi e kore e whakaae kia purua e te whakawā, e te tikanga rānei.

Ki a Xander rāua ko Leia-Lorena: ko tēnei whakapapa tō kōrua mahere. He tuhinga pūmau o te kaha e mau ana i ō kōrua ingoa, me te auahatanga e rere ana i roto i a kōrua. Ahakoa te tawhiti, ngā wero rānei e pā mai, he wāhanga kōrua o tētahi mekameka e kore e taea te wāhi. Puritia tō kōrua whānau kia tata, nā te mea ko te kōrero e hangaia ana i tēnei rā te tuku ihotanga mā kōrua āpōpō.
