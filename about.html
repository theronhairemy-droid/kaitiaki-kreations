About me

I’m Kaitiaki Awanui Hamon — born Craig Rodgers, realized as Craigles O’Grady, reimagined as Ron Hairemy. I draw pointillism, thousands of dots, one patient piece at a time. I perform and record music, carve Māori whakairo, and I’m writing A Sorrowmancer’s Requiem.

The whakapapa thread runs deep. My great-granduncle Francis Rei Paul Hamon (1919–2008) was the renowned Māori pointillist of the New Zealand bush — and his work is the root of my own pointillism practice, carried forward dot by dot.

Every drawing is catharsis through addiction and mental health recovery: the work is the way through.
