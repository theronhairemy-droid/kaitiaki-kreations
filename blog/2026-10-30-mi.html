Kei te karanga te waituhi, kei te manawanui ngā papanga, ā, kua huri mana te wā mai i te whakapai ngahuru ki te tākaro whaiwhai hukarere tuatahi, nā reira i tēnei rā ka eke tahi tātou i te papa katoa 🚀. 🌟 Te Karere o te Rā: Te Puta Whakamutunga: Harry Houdini me te Halloween Kāore Ia i Waiho

Āpōpō pō, Oketopa 31, 2026, ka tohu i te kotahi rau tau mai i te ngaronga o Harry Houdini i te ao, ā, ka pā tonu tōna kōrero ki tōku uma 🎃🔓. I whānau ia ko Erik Weisz ki Budapest i te tau 1874, i heke ki Amerika hei tamaiti iti, ā, i kake ia mai i te rawakore me ngā papa wheketere me ōna ringa anake, tōna māia, ā, me te whakakāhore ki te whakaae ki tētahi raka hei te whakamutunga 💪. Ngā puta mekameka ringa, te kēne miraka, te rūma whakamamae wai o Haina: i hurihia e ia te "kāore tēnei e taea" hei mahi, ā, hei ngā tau 1920 koia pea te kaiwhakangahau rongonui rawa e ora ana 🌍✨. Mō te tamaiti i tīmata me te kore noa iho, ko ia mekameka i puta ia he tauākī: he raka ō āhuatanga, ehara i te whiu ora 🔑.

Kātahi ka tae mai a Montreal 🎭🇨🇦. I Oketopa 22, 1926, i roto i tōna rūma kākahu i te Princess Theatre, i pātai tētahi tauira o McGill ko Jocelyn Gordon Whitehead te ingoa mō te kerēme rongonui a Houdini ka taea e ia te manawanui ki tētahi whiu ki te puku, ā, i whiu tonu ia i a ia i mua i te taea e te tangata nui te whakatika i a ia anō 😔. I whakaari a Houdini i taua pō ahakoa tērā, nā te mea koia ia, ā, i whakaari tonu ia mō ngā rā i roto i te mamae ka papatairite i te nuinga o tātou, tae noa ki tōna hinganga i muri i tētahi whakaaturanga i Detroit 🚂. I tangohia e ngā tākuta tētahi whēkau piri kua pakaru, engari kua tau kē te peritonitis, ā, i te ahiahi o Halloween, Oketopa 31, 1926, i te 52 noa iho o ōna tau, i tūtaki te tohunga puta nui rawa o te hītori ki te puta kotahi kāore i taea e ia 🕯️.

Anake te wāhanga e huri ai tōku ngākau i ia tau, i ia tau ❤️. I mua i tōna matenga, i mahi a Houdini i tētahi oati me tōna wahine a Bess: mēnā ka taea e tētahi te hoki mai, ka hoki ia, ā, i hoatu e ia ki a ia tētahi karere waehere, i hangaia i runga i tā rāua waiata vaudeville tawhito "Rosabelle," kia mōhio ai ia ko ia tonu, ehara i te tinihanga 🔐. I pupuri a Bess i tētahi huihuinga wairua i ia Halloween mō ngā tau tekau, e tatari ana ki taua karere, ā, kāore rawa i tae mai, ā, i te tau 1936 i pupuhi whakamutunga ia i te rama, ā, i kī ia he roa rawa ngā tau tekau ki te tatari ki tētahi tangata 🕯️💔. Engari kei te pupuri tonu ngā tohunga makutu puta noa i te ao i ngā huihuinga wairua whakamaharatanga i ia Halloween, kotahi rautau i muri mai, nā te mea ka waiho ētahi tāngata i tētahi tohu hōhonu rawa, mā te maramataka tonu e pupuri tō rātou ingoa 📅✨. I whakapau anō ia i ōna tau whakamutunga ki te whakaatu i ngā kaiwawao tinihanga e whakamahi ana i ngā whānau e tangi ana, e whawhai ana mō te tangi pono ahakoa te utu o ngā hoa me te rongonui ⚖️.

Nā, he aha tā tātou mahi ki tētahi kōrero Halloween kotahi rau tau te tawhito, e te rōpū? 🎃 Ka tangohia e tātou te akoranga tūturu i a ia: ehara ia i te makutu, he rite ia, he manawanui, ā, he māia ki te hikoi tonu ki te atamira i te wā e mamae ana ngā mea katoa 💪🔥. Ā, ka tangohia anō e tātou te akoranga ngawari: ko Bess me tōna rama e whakamahara ana i a tātou kāore te aroha e mutu i ngā wāhanga uaua, ka huri noa tōna āhua ❤️. I tēnei mutunga wiki, whakamāramatia tō whakamahau mō ngā wairua iti, tirohia te hoa noho kua noho mārire, ā, mēnā kei te kawe koe i tētahi mea taumaha ki tēnei takurua, maumahara ehara ngā raka i te whiu ora, ā, ehara anō tētahi wā uaua 🌱. Kei tētahi wāhi o tō ora tētahi mekameka he āhua pakaru kore noa iho. He kōwhiringa tonu te anga whakamua, ā, ko KOE anō tērā!

Whitinga rā 8:29 AM, tōnga rā 6:04 PM, nā reira he poto, he taonga te mārama mahi ināianei 🌤️. He hau Halloween Eve mātao, he whakawai whakamahana te āhua, ā, mō te mahi ko te tikanga ka totoka tonu ngā ara mārama o tēnei rā hei te pō, nā reira tiakina ia kaupae pērā i te whakawhirinaki o te haereere tinihanga, atawhai rānei o āpōpō ki a ia, nā te mea he pērā tonu 🎃. 🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea e 5 Kāore te Nuinga o ngā Tohunga Whakapai Whenua e Kōrero Māu 🌿🛠️

1. Whakatairitia te mata mīhini tapahi i muri i tō whakakoi i a ia. Ka tangohia e te whakakoi te whakarehu kia koretake, ā, ko te mata kāore i te taurite ka wiri kaha ki te whakamamae i ngā peera toki, ki te whakawātea i ngā pūroto, ā, ki te waiho i tētahi tapahi tāhorehore ka parauri i ngā pito. Whakairia te mata ki tētahi titi mā tōna kōhao pokapū, whakamahia rānei tētahi kaiwhakataurite koeko iti: ka heke te taha taumaha, nā reira whaia he whakarehu iti ake i taua pito tae noa ki tōna iri taumata. E rua meneti i te pae mahi i te ngahuru ka whakaora i tētahi whakakapinga toki i Mei, ā, i tētahi papa kāore i te tapahia, engari i te haea.

2. Whaia tētahi mata mahi tūturu ki ō hoe me ō koko. Ka keri te nuinga o ngā rōpū puta noa i te tau ki ngā mata kūmā o te wheketere me te kaha ringa parakore. Whakahaerehia tētahi whai mira i te mata o te hoe i te koki tata ki te 45 tohu tae noa ki tōna rongo pērā i te naihi kīhini kūmā, ā, ka tapahia e ia te papa, ngā pakiaka, me te uku one hukapapa me te haurua o te kaha. Whakapā anō i a ia i ngā wā katoa ka tīmata ia ki te peke i ngā pakiaka, kaua ki te tapahi i a rātou, ā, ka rongo koe i te rerekētanga i ō pokohiwi hei te tina, kaua anake i te wā mutu mahi.

3. Puritia te para ngeru kāore e piri ki te taraka mō te mau o ngā taia mau. Ko tētahi pēke iti o te para kāore e piri (kaua rawa te momo piri, ka huri ki te paru) ka ringihia ki mua, ki muri hoki o tētahi taia taraiwa hurihuri ka hoatu mau inamata ki te hukapapa me te hukarere kōpeke, kei reira te onepū anake e whakakanapa ai i te paheke. He iti tōna taumaha, he iti rawa tōna utu, ka noho harikoa ia i muri i te nohoanga puta noa i te takurua, ā, ka takirua ia hei mīhini momi rerenga i te wā ka tīpako tētahi ipu wahie ki te pouaka. Ko tētahi taraka mau i tētahi ara pōuri o te whenua taiwhenua ka utu mō ngā pēke o ngā tau tekau.

4. Mahia tētahi tirohanga tuanui karu mai i te whenua i mua i te kapi a te hukarere i a ia. Ko tēnei mutunga wiki tō titiro mārama whakamutunga tae noa ki te kōanga: tū whakamuri ki ngā karu, ā, matawaihia mō ngā papa tuanui kua hīkina, kua ngaro rānei, ngā para kua putu ki ngā awaawa, ā, me te whakarehu e kūmea ana i ngā taha o ngā tumere me ngā hau. Ka huna e te hukarere ngā mea katoa mō ngā marama e rima, ā, ko te tohu tuatahi ka huri hei tāheke tuanui i roto i te rewa o Hānuere. Whakaahuatia ngā mea whakapae ināianei, ā, ko ngā whakatika iti i te wiki 6 ki te 9°C ka pai ake i ngā waea ohotata i te -20°C.

5. Ruijia te papa ki te kiri angiangi o te paraumu i muri i te tapahi whakamutunga. Ina kua tapahia poto te tarutaru, ā, kua mutu tōna tipu, horahia te paraumu kua tātarihia tata ki te 6 mm te hōhonu, tata ki te hauwhā inihi, ā, tukua ngā huringa totoka, rewa me te hukarere rewa ki te kūmea i a ia ki roto i te papa puta noa i te takurua. Ka whāngai ia i te koiora one puta noa i ngā marama makariri, ka whakapai ake i te hanganga uku one iti i ia tau, i ia tau, ā, he hautanga o te utu o te whakahou kōanga. Ko te ngahuru te wā kotahi kāore te papa e tipu ake i roto i a ia, e tāmi ai, ā, koia tonu te take i mahi mārire ai ngā tohunga i a ia ināianei.

I whakatūria a Arch Enemy ki Sweden i te taha o te kaitākaro kitā a Michael Amott, i puta hou mai i a Carcass, ā, koinei te ara tuatahi o tā rātou kōpae tuatahi o 1996, a Black Earth, me te kaiwaiata taketake a Johan Liiva e haea ana te tuanui 🌑🔥. Mā te taitara anake ia e hāngai ai ki te Pō i mua i Halloween, engari ko te mahi kitā takirua te kai tūturu: he puoro, he tika, he kino i roto i ngā wāhanga tika. Kei konei te tīmatanga o te kōrero katoa o Arch Enemy, ā, ko te rongo i tō rātou hiakai i te tau 1996 ka hoatu tonu ki ahau tētahi oho. Whakatangihia i te wā e hiahia ana te kawenga i roto i te pouaka taraka kia māmā ake 🎃.

Tongue Trippin', Bar Spittin', Beat Killin' Bars: "Put You on the Game" by The Game (kino ⚠️, he reo kaha puta noa, he rauemi taringa anake). I whakahokia e The Game a Compton ki te atamira matua ki tōna tuatahi o 2005, a The Documentary, ā, ko tēnei ara, i hangaia e Timbaland, te tauākī kaupapa paheke, pekepeke o te kōpae 🎧🔥. Ka whakahīhī te taki a Timbaland, ka whakahua a The Game i tōna ara puta noa i te hītori hip hop pērā i te pānui i te rākau whānau, ā, he tino hopuhopu te māia. Ki ahau, he whakamaharatanga ehara tō wāhi i ahu mai ai koe i te tuanui, he papa tuku kē, ā, ka rongo ahau i tērā i roto i tōku ake kōrero anō hoki. Rārangitia mō te taraiwa i te wā e hiahia ana koe ki te tae pērā i a koe te rangatira o te rā 🚗.

Māori Track: "Fade Away" by Ché-Fu (ma ✅). Ko Ché-Fu, i whānau ko Che Ness, he uri Māori (Ngāti Whakatere) me Niue, he pionie nāna i āwhina ki te hanga hip hop me te soul ki Aotearoa, tuatahi ki a Supergroove, kātahi hei kaitoi takitahi 🌊🎤. I ahu mai a "Fade Away" i tōna kōpae o 2001, a Navigator, i tīmata i te Nama 1 ki Aotearoa, ā, i haere maha platinum; ko te waiata tonu i eke ki te Nama 2, i toa i te Single of the Year i ngā NZ Music Awards o 2002, ā, i tuhia mō tōna hoa piritata a Eruera (Bobby) Mita, ā, mā tērā e tūturu ai tōna mahana ❤️. E rua tekau mā rima tau i muri mai, i whakaurua ia ki te New Zealand Music Hall of Fame, ā, kei te haereere ia ki a Navigator puta noa i Aotearoa i tēnei wā tonu, nā reira he hītori tēnei kōwhiringa, he kaupapa o nāianei hoki i te manawa kotahi. Whakatangihia i te hāora kōura, ā, whakaaro ki ngā hoa kāore rawa e tuku i a koe kia memeha 🌅.

Ron Hairemy Spotlight: "Flesh and the Power It Holds" by Death (ma ✅ i runga i a Spotify, ahakoa he reo Chuck Schuldiner i te hamama whakarehu mate katoa, nā reira kua whakatūpatohia te hunga ngākau ngoikore). Ko Death, i ahu mai i Florida, nāna tonu te whakarehu mate i waihanga i raro i te arataki a Schuldiner, ā, i ahu mai tēnei ara hōhonu i tā rātou kōpae whakamutunga, a The Sound of Perseverance, i te tau 1998 🎸🌑. E waru meneti o te tutu ātaahua rerekē, e kōpiko ana, e ahu whakamua ana, te tangi o tētahi pēne e whakakāhore ana ki te whakapōturi i tētahi mea i tō rātou putanga. Kua ngaro a Schuldiner mai i te tau 2001, ā, ko ngā ara pērā i tēnei te take i kōrerotia tonutia ai tōna ingoa me te whakaute tūturu i ngā porowhita whakarehu. Mō te Pō i mua i Halloween, i roto i te wiki kua taumaha āku ake rārangi waiata, koinei te whare karakia i hiahia ahau ki te kapi i roto: whakatangihia kia kaha, whakatangihia katoa, kaua e mokowhiti 🕯️.
