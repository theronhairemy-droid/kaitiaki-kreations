🌟 Te Karere o te Rā Nei: Te Kōtiro i Tono ai ia he Kura: Shannen Koostachin

He kōtiro Cree a Shannen Koostachin nō Attawapiskat First Nation, i runga i te takutai o James Bay o Ontario 🇨🇦🌊. I haere ia ki J.R. Nakogee Elementary, hāunga kāore he kura tūturu hei haerenga: kua whakakāhoretia te whare, ā, ko tōna mātauranga katoa i tū i roto i tētahi rārangi o ngā whare kawe paru e tū ana i te taha o tētahi wāhi para paitini 🏚️. Kāore rawa ia i tōna oranga i takahi i roto i tētahi kura tika, ā, kāore hoki ngā tamariki e haere ake ana i muri i a ia 😔. I te wā i te tau 8 ia, i kite ia i ngā tamariki tau 5 pukumahi e whakarēre noa ana, e wehe ana, nā te mea i kōrero ngā āhuatanga ki a rātou i ia rā, i ia rā, te nui o te uara i tukua e te pūnaha ki a rātou 💔.

Anei te wāhanga e hanga ana i a ia hei whawhai, ehara i te kōrero pōuri noa 🥊. Kua oati te kāwanatanga ā-motu ki Attawapiskat he kura hou ehara i te kotahi, ehara i te rua, engari e toru ngā wā, ā, i te tau 2007 kua hoki whakamuri hoki i te oati tuatoru 📉. Nō reira i whakatau tētahi 13 tau ki te mahi i te mahi manaaki a te kāwanatanga, nui te reo: i whakarewa a Shannen me ōna hoa ākonga i te Attawapiskat School Campaign, e kawe ana i a Students Helping Students ki YouTube me Facebook i mua i te noho hei mea noa mā ngā tamariki 📱🔥. I te tau 2008 i haere ia ki Ottawa, ā, i noho i te ritenga atu o te Minita o ngā Take Inia tonu, nāna i kī ki a ia kāore i te nui te pūtea. I kī atu ia ki a ia kāore rawa ia e whakarēre, nā te mea "he wā te kura mō ngā moemoeā, ā, e tika ana tēnei mā ia tamaiti" ✨. Ko taua rārangi, nō tētahi taiohi i ngā whare kawe, i whakaingoatia ia mō te International Children's Peace Prize 🏅.

I tipu tonu te pakanga i muri i tōna hokinga ki te kāinga, ngā ākonga puta noa i te whenua e tuhi reta ana, e hanga ataata ana, e whakakāhore ana kia waiho tētahi pai whare kawe i runga i te putunga para hei mutunga o te kōrero ✊📚. Kātahi, i te tau 2010, i mate a Shannen i tētahi aituā waka i Highway 11 e tata ana ki Temagami. He 15 ōna tau 🕯️. I mate ia e tatari tonu ana mō te kura i oati ai ki a ia i tōna tamarikitanga katoa. I whakatau tōna whānau me ōna hoa i roto i tō rātou pōuri kāore te nuinga o ngā pakeke e mahi i te wā hāneanea: i tiki anō rātou i te whawhai, ā, i Noema 2010 i whakarewa anō rātou i raro i tōna ingoa, Shannen's Dream 💙. I Pēpuere 2012, i kawea e ngā rangatahi o ngā Iwi Tuatahi tōna kōrero ki te United Nations Committee on the Rights of the Child i Geneva 🌍, ā, i Pēpuere 27, 2012, i pāhi te House of Commons i te motini Shannen's Dream me te kotahi o ngā reo, ia rōpū katoa, e karanga ana kia utua ngā kura o ngā Iwi Tuatahi i runga i te tūnga ōrite 🇨🇦.

Ā, te kura? I tīmata te hanga o Kattawapiskak Elementary i Hune 22, 2012, te rā tonu i puta ai a Shannen i te kura 🎓, ā, i whakatuwhera i ōna kūaha i Hepetema 8, 2014, he kura tūturu, me ngā pakitara tūturu, mō ngā tamariki o Attawapiskat 🏫🎉. Kua whakaingoatia ia ināianei hei tētahi o ngā 150 Great Canadians, he tohu whakamaharatanga ki a ia i te taha wai o New Liskeard, ā, i kōrero te National Film Board i tōna kōrero i Hi-Ho Mistahey!, ko tōna tikanga "E aroha ana ahau ki a koe mō ake tonu" i te reo Cree 🎬. Engari anei te mutunga pono: kei te haere tonu a Shannen's Dream i tēnei rā mā te First Nations Child and Family Caring Society, nā te mea ko te āputa pūtea i whakaingoatia e ia, he mano iti iho mō ia ākonga i ngā kura porowini, kāore anō kia kati katoa ⚖️. He tino māmā tāna karanga mahi, ā, nō tātou ināianei: kei te kōrero ngā tamariki e tata ana ki a koe i ngā mea e hiahiatia ana e rātou. Whakapono ki a rātou i te wā tuatahi, ā, kaua e whakarēre 📣. He kōwhiringa tonu te anga whakamua, ā, ko KOE hoki tērā!

Ka waea tētahi wahine kakaho ki tōna hoa tāne me ngā roimata katoa 😭📞. "Kei te ngana ahau ki te mahi i tēnei panga whakaahua, ā, kāore e kitea e ahau ngā wāhanga e rua e uru tahi ana! Kāore he wāhanga tapa kotahi! KĀORE HE MEA!" 🧩💢 Ka kī tōna hoa tāne, he mārie pēnei i te tangata ka taea, "Pai, he manawa hōhonu. He aha te mea e tika ana mō te panga?" "E aroha… whakatā, inu i tētahi kapu tī ☕, kātahi ka whakahokia e tāua ēnei Frosties katoa ki roto i te pouaka." 🥣🤣

🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea e 5 Kāore te Nuinga o ngā Kaiwhakapaipai Whenua Ngaio e Kōrero ki a Koe 🌿🛠️

1. Kua TUWHERA te matapihi tapahi elm o Edmonton: Oketopa 1 ki Maehe 31 anake 🌳
Ko te nuinga o ngā kiritaki he whakamuri ō rātou whakaaro, ā, ka tono mō te mahi elm i te raumati, koirā tonu te mea kotahi e āraia tōtika ana e te ture ā-rohe. E ārai ana a Edmonton i te tapahi elm mai i Āperira 1 ki Hepetema 30, nā te mea he hohe ngā pītara kiri elm i taua wā, ā, ka aro ki ngā tapahi hou, e hora ana i te harore mate elm Tatimana. Kei te pupuri tonu a Alberta i tētahi o ngā kānawehi elm kore-mate whakamutunga nui o te whenua nui, tata ki te 42,000 elm tūmatanui i tēnei tāone anake, ā, ko te matapihi takurua tētahi take nui. Ngā ture e rua e whai ana ngā ngaio kāore e pātaihia: whakamakukū i ō taputapu i waenga i ngā rākau elm (70% waipiro, methyl hydrate rānei), ā, maka atu te rakau elm ināianei mā te tahu, mā te kawe rānei ki te Edmonton Waste Management Centre. Kaua rawa, kaua rawa e tāpuke i te wahie elm: he hōtēra pītara tērā me te tohu wātea e mura ana 🪲.

2. Kaua rawa e whakaranu i te onepū ki te uku hei whakatika: kei te hanga pereki koe, ehara i te oneone 🧱
He āhua ahupūngao: he tino pai te uku, he taratara te onepū, whakaranuhia, ā, tūtaki i waenganui. Ko te mea e whiwhi pono ana koe i te tauine māra he adobe. Ko te uku me tētahi rahinga iti o te onepū me te wai te tohutao tūturu mō ngā pereki me te cob, ā, ko tētahi moenga i whakatikaina pērā ka mārō ake i te uku i tīmata ai koe. Kia tino whakarerekē i te kakano o te uku me te onepū me nui ake te onepū i te haurua o te rahinga katoa, e parau ōrite ana tētahi putu te hōhonu, he tini tana rauemi tērā me tētahi hanganga oneone kua whakangaromia mō tō raruraru. Ko te whakatika e mahi pono ana he mea rauropi: he wairākau kua mahia ki roto, kua whakamākū ki runga, tau i muri i te tau, nā te mea mā te koiora oneone e whakapiri ngā matūriki uku hei kongakonga e rere ana, e manawa ana. Mō ngā paparanga rerenga wai me ngā hononga papa te onepū, ehara mō te whakakakara i te uku 🚫.

3. Whakamau anō, whakatika ngā kūaha taiapa INĀIANEI, i mua i te raka a te haupapa piki i a rātou kia kōaro 🚪
Ko tētahi kūaha e tārere ana, e kukume ana, e ngaro ana rānei i tōna raka i Oketopa he whakatika e rima meneti i te wā e noho tonu ana ngā pou i te oneone e taea te mahi: whakaū, whakanohonoho anō rānei i ngā inaki, whakateka i te anga, whakakikī i te tautoko hauroki, whakarite te raka ki te wāhi e kati pono ai te kūaha. Tatari tae noa ki te mātaratara o te whenua, ā, ka whiwhi te haupapa piki i te pōti, ka neke te pou i tōna ake wātaka, ka raka kōaro, ā, kāore te kūaha e kati, kāore rānei e tuwhera tae noa ki te kōanga. Mā te aki i tētahi kūaha mātaratara e pakaru ai ngā rēra, e kōaro tūturu ai ngā anga. He nekehanga tāpiri i a koe i reira: tirohia te āwhio piu, ā, whakawāteatia ngā mea katoa e hiahia ana te kūaha ki te kukume i te hukarere tuatahi ❄️.

4. Tangohia tētahi mahere ātārangi rau-kore i tēnei wiki, i mua i te whakamahere i tētahi whakatō kōanga kotahi 📸
Ināianei, me te kānawehi ki raro, ko te tirohanga pono anake ka whiwhi koe ki te mārama o tētahi iari tae noa ki te ngahuru e whai ake. Hikoi i te rawa i te ata, i waenganui o te rā, ā, i te mutunga o te ahiahi, whakaahuatia ia moenga: ka tae te rā rau-kore ki ngā wāhi e puritia pōuri ana e te kānawehi o Hūrae mō ngā marama, ā, ka taea ngā moenga tonga te rere i ngā wiki i mua i te toharite o te iari hei te kōanga. Mā taua mahere e kōrero ki a koe te wāhi e rewa tuatahi ai te hukarere, te wāhi e mātaratara anō ai te wai rewa hei pepa huka i ia pō, ā, ko tēhea moenga "rā katoa" he moenga ātārangi wāhi tūturu me te PR pai. Whakamaheretia ngā wehewehenga kōanga, ngā tāpiri puku, me ngā whakamātautau pana Rohe mai i te mahere rau-kore, kaua mai i tētahi maharatanga raumati e whakapai ana i te iari ☀️.

5. Utua ngā horoi ngahuru mā te kānawehi me te kawenga rau, kaua mā te rahi o te papakumu 💰
Ka taea e ngā wāhi e rua te ine ōrite i te pepa, ā, e toru ngā wā rerekē i te mahi horoi. Ko te wāhi tuwhera me tētahi rākau taitamariki he pupuhi-me-haere; ko te tapuwae ōrite i raro i tētahi kānawehi elm me te spruce pakeke, i tētahi kokonga e tāpuke ana te hau i ngā rau a te katoa ki te taiapa, he rā katoa me ngā tāpora. Nō reira whakataua te kānawehi, kaua te tarutaru: tatauhia ngā rākau pakeke, tuhia ngā momo (ka whakaheke te spruce i ngā ngira ki ngā moenga i te ngahuru katoa, ka pupuri ngā oke i ngā rau kia tōmuri, ā, ka whakaheke ā-wāhanga), ā, tirohia ngā kokonga taiapa me ngā poka matapihi e tāpuke hōhonu rawa ai ngā rau rere. Utua mā te rahinga rau whakatau, haere hāora rānei me tētahi tepe kua kōrerotia, ā, kōrerotia ko tēhea e mahia ana e koe i mua i te tīmatanga o te pupuhi tuatahi.
