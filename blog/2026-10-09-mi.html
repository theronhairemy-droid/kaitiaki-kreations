🌟 Te Karere o te Rā Nei: Te Rōpū Puoro i Peti ki a Ia Anō

Kua whakaaro ahau mō ngā tamariki tokotoru o Willowdale Toronto 🇨🇦, a Alex Lifeson, a Geddy Lee, me tētahi kaiwhakatangi pahū ko John Rutsey tōna ingoa, nā rātou i tīmata tētahi rōpū puoro ko Rush te ingoa i te tau 1968, ā, i whakapau rātou i ngā tau e ono ki te whakatangi i ngā pāpara me ngā kanikani kura tuarua mō te tata kore tangata 🎸😅. Kātahi i te tau 1974 ka huri te tūru pahū ki ētahi ringa kē, ā, ka hikoi mai a Neil Peart o Hamilton, he tamaiti pukapuka i haere ki London ki te angitu, i hinga, ā, i hoki mai ki te kāinga me tana hiku i waenganui i ōna waewae 🥁📚. I mīharo te rōpū ki ana whakatangi, ā, he tangata taea e ia te TUHI, nā reira ko ia hoki tō rātou kaitito kupu ✍️🔥. I haere a Fly By Night ki te platinum, engari kātahi ka hinga kinotia tā rātou rekoata tuatoru, ka tīmata te kamupene ki te whakakoi i te toki 📉😬. I hiahia a Mercury Records ki ngā waiata irirangi. He mea kē anō tā te rōpū i hiahia ai.

Nā reira i mahi rātou i te whakatau tino pakeke i roto i te hītori toka o Canada 💪🇨🇦. Engari i te tākaro haumaru, i rekoata a Rush i a 2112, he waiata pakiwaitara pūtaiao 20 meneti mō tētahi anamata e whakakāhoretia ai te puoro tonu, ā, ka kitea e tētahi tangata he kitā 🚫🎸. I rite te kamupene mō tētahi hinganga whakamutu mahi… ā, i haere ki te platinum toru 🏆🏆🏆. Kātahi ka tae mai a Permanent Waves me a Moving Pictures, me The Spirit of Radio, Tom Sawyer, Limelight, YYZ 📻🔥. Tokotoru ngā tāngata. Kāore he kaitohutohu āhua, kāore he rōpū arotahi, kāore he whakararu, ko rātou tokotoru anake e pai ake ana ki te mahi i a RĀTOU, he kaha ake, mō ngā tau e whā tekau 🥁🎸🎤. He tekau miriona rekoata i muri mai, ko rātou te rōpū puoro koromiko tino arohaina o te ao 🌍💛.

Ā, kātahi ka patua kinotia ake e te oranga taua kaiwhakatangi pahū i tā tētahi e tika ai kia patua 💔. I te tau 1997, i mate a Selena, te tamāhine a Neil, 19 noa iho, i tētahi aituā waka. I raro i te tau kotahi i muri mai, i mate tana wahine a Jacqueline i te mate pukupuku 😔🕊️. I kī a Neil ki a Geddy rāua ko Alex kua MUTU ia, kua whakatā, kua oti, kātahi ka mahi ia i te mea tino Neil Peart e taea te whakaaro: i piki ia ki runga i tētahi motopaika, ā, i eke ko ia anake puta noa i North and Central America me tana pōuri, mō ngā mano maero 🏍️🌎. I tuhia katoatia e ia ki Ghost Rider: Travels on the Healing Road 📖, ā, maero ki maero, ka hoki mai te puoro. I marena anō ia, i hono anō ki te rōpū i te tau 2001, ā, i haruru whakamuri a Rush mō tētahi atu tekau tau me te haurua 🔥🥁.

He mīharo ngā mea i mahue iho: he Āpiha o te Order of Canada i te tau 1996 🍁🎖️, te Canadian Music Hall of Fame i te tau 1994, te Rock and Roll Hall of Fame i te tau 2013, ā, ko aua tāngata tokotoru anō mō ngā tau e 41 karapīpiti, kāore he whakakapi, kāore he porowhita huihuinga anō, kāore he raruraru 🤝. I mahue mai a Neil i a tātou i te 7 o Hānuere, 2020, whai muri i tētahi whawhai tūmataiti e toru tau ki te mate pukupuku roro, kāore rawa ia i hoatu ki tētahi atu kia mauria 🕊️🥁. Ā, i tēnei tau, i pānuitia e Geddy rāua ko Alex te haerenga Fifty Something, me te manaakitanga o te whānau o Neil, ki te kawe anō i ēnei waiata ki te rori kotahi atu anō 🥹🎸. Nā reira, anei taku mahi kāinga mōu mō te mutunga wiki o te Whakawhetai: whakatangihia a Tom Sawyer, waea atu ki tētahi kua roa koe e whakaaro ana ki te waea atu, ā, kōrero atu he aha tō rātou hiranga ki a koe i mua i te taenga o te korukoru ki te tēpu 📞🦃.

Ā, maumahara, he kōwhiringa I NGĀ WĀ KATOA te haere whakamua, ā, ko KOE HOKI TĒRĀ! 💛🚀

🤣😅😄 Te Kata Mahi Whenua o ia Rā

Ka haere tētahi poraka iti ki tētahi pēke, ā, ka peke ki te tēpu pūtea taurewa 🐸💼

"Kia ora," e mea ana te poraka. "Ko Kermit Jagger taku ingoa, ā, kei te hiahia ahau ki tētahi pūtea taurewa ki te hoko i tētahi waka paparanga rengarenga."

Ka kemo te kaiwhakahaere moni, he wahine ko Patricia Whack tōna ingoa. "E pā, me whai taurangi mātou."

Ka toro te poraka ki tana pōkete poraka iti, ā, ka tango mai i tētahi arewhana uku iti 🐘✨ "Tēnei. Ko tēnei anake tāku."

Ka haria e Ms. Whack te arewhana ki tana kaiwhakahaere. "He poraka kei raro e hiahia ana ki tētahi pūtea taurewa, ā, ko tēnei anake tāna taurangi… mea. He aha rā tēnei MEA?"

Ka titiro kotahi te kaiwhakahaere, ka menemene, ā, ka mea:

"He knick-knack tēnei, Patty Whack. Hoatu he pūtea taurewa ki te poraka. He Rolling Stone tōna pāpā koroua." 🎸🐸😂

🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea e 5 Kāore e Kōrerohia e te Nuinga o ngā Kaimahi Whenua Ngaio

1. Whakamakuku hōhonu i ō rākau i mua i te hukapapa. Anei te mea kāore tētahi e whakapono tae noa ki tana whakakapinga i tētahi rākau paina mate: ko te nuinga o te mate rākau takurua i Edmonton ehara i te makariri, he matewai. Ka haere ngā rākau ki te takurua he maroke, ka raka te whenua tio i te makukutanga iti e toe ana, ā, ka unu te hau i te toenga mai i ngā ngira me ngā manga puta noa i te wā. I mua i te tio totoka o te whenua, hoatu ki ia rākau he inu roa, pōturi, hōhonu, he hāora maha ki te ngongo whakamakuku pōturi i te rārangi maturuturu, ehara i te tauhiuhi rima meneti. Ko te rākau kua whakamakukuhia he rākau ka oho i Āperira.

2. Pupuhia ngā rārangi whakamakuku i mua i te Rāhoroi. Ko te rārangi whakamakuku kua utaina e tūtaki ana ki te tio pakeke he pōma paipa me te mataara. Ka rahi te wai, ka pakaru te PVC me ngā Ārai rerenga whakamuri, ā, kāore koe e kite tae noa ki te tīmatanga o te kōanga, i te taenga mai o te pire. I tēnei rā, i te wā kei runga tonu i te kore, whakahaerehia te kōpeke puta noa i ia rohe, ā, whakatahetahe i te rerenga whakamuri. Kāore te hukapapa o te Rāhoroi e aro ki tō wātaka, nā reira patua i mua.

3. Wetekina, ā, whakatahetahe i ngā ngongo māra i tēnei pō. Ka utu te tangata i te moni tūturu mō tēnei i ia tau kotahi. Ko te ngongo i waiho kia piri, kia kī i te wai, ka tio whakamuri puta noa i te kōrere, ā, ka pakaru te paipa i roto i te pakitara, ā, kāore taua pakaru e pānui i a ia anō tae noa ki te waipuke o te rewa kōanga i te papa o raro. I tēnei pō: tutakina te wai, wetekina ia ngongo, whakatahetahetia, waiwirihia, whakairihia. E rua meneti mō ia ngongo. Ko te inihua iti rawa te utu i te rangitaki katoa.

4. Kaua e tapahi i ngā tipu pua kōanga i te ngahuru. Whakatakotoria ngā tapahi mai i ngā lilac, ngā forsythia, ngā mock-orange. Ka whakatakoto ngā rakau iti pua kōanga i ō rātou pua ki te mutunga o te raumati, ki ngā rākau kua reira kē, tapahia ināianei, ā, kua tapahia e koe te whakaaturanga katoa o te kōanga e tū mai nei. He māmā te ture: ka tapahia ngā tipu pua kōanga i muri tonu i tō rātou puanga, kaua rawa i mua. Whakapaia i Hune, whakawhetai ki a koe anō i Mei.

5. Tapahia te pona pango i te wā e tahanga ana ngā manga. Ko te pona pango, aua pupuhitanga pango kino e rite ana ki te tā ki ngā rākau here me ngā paramu o Edmonton, ka horapa i ōna puaaheke i te ua kōanga. Ko te wā moe, i te wā e tahanga ana te rākau, ā, ka taea e koe te kite i ia manga pangia, te wā e whai ai koe. Tapahia ki raro rawa i te pona, 15 to 20 cm ki te rākau mā, whakakore i ngā mate o tō mata i waenganui i ngā tapahi, ā, tahuna, purua rānei ngā mea ka heke iho. Kaua rawa e wairākau. He hāora kotahi o te mahi kaitirotiro moe ināianei ka whakaora i te rākau katoa i muri mai.
