🌟 Te Karere o Tēnei Rā: Mahia Tētahi Mea: Te Koha Whakamutunga a Gord Downie

Me kōrero atu au ki a koe mō Gord Downie 🇨🇦🎤, te kaiwaiata matua o The Tragically Hip, te kaitito whakamānawa o ngā raumati katoa o ngā tāone iti o Kanata, he tangata nā tōna reo i whakatipu tētahi whakatupuranga katoa o mātou 🌅. I Mei 2016 i whiwhi ia i te tātaritanga kāore he tangata e ora ai: he mate pukupuku roro whakamate, he glioblastoma 🧠💔. E 52 ōna tau, i te tihi o ōna pūkenga, ā, kātahi anō te karaka ka tīmata ki te tīwaha ⏳. He tokomaha ngā tāngata kua noho puku. I kaha ake te reo o Gord 🔊.

I kawea e ia a The Hip ki tētahi haerenga whakamutunga puta noa i Kanata 🎸🇨🇦, i pau ngā tīkiti mai i Victoria ki Kingston i te wā e tango haere ana te mate pukupuku i a ia i ia pō, ā, i Ākuhata 20, 2016, i tōna tāone kāinga o Kingston, i whakatangihia e ia te whakaaturanga whakamutunga ki tētahi whenua e pupuri ana i tōna manawa 📺❤️. I mātakitaki ngā miriona. Ā, i waenganui o tōna poroporoaki, me te Pirimia e noho ana i roto i te mano, i tahuri atu a Gord i te rama nui rawa o tōna oranga mai i a ia anō, ki te mahi a Kanata ki ngā iwi Taketake, ā, i kī atu ia ki a Justin Trudeau, i runga i te pouaka whakaata ora, me mahi ia i tētahi mea mō tēnā 🪶🔥. He tangata e mate ana, e whakamahi ana i tōna poroporoaki hei mīhini whakakaha reo mō ngā tāngata i warewaretia e tēnei whenua mō ngā tau 150. Tukua tēnā kia noho ki tō uma mō tētahi hēkona 💓.

Ko te mea i mau i a ia ko te kōrero mō Chanie Wenjack 🕯️, he tama Ojibwe 12 tau te pakeke i kawea ki te Kura Noho Taketake Cecilia Jeffrey i Kenora, i oma i Oketopa 1966 e ngana ana ki te hīkoi 600 kiromita ki te kāinga, ki te whānau i tangohia atu ai ia 🚶‍♂️🌲. I kitea te tinana o Chanie i te taha o ngā ara rerewē i Oketopa 22, 1966, i mate ia i te hiakai me te pānga ki te māeke, he tamaiti, ko ia anake, e ngana ana ki te hoki ki te kāinga 💔❄️. I rongo tuatahi a Gord i te kōrero mai i tōna tuakana a Mike, ā, kāore noa ia i taea te tuku. I hurihia e ia ki Secret Path: he pukaemi, he pukapuka whakaahua me Jeff Lemire, he kiriata pakiwaituhi, i tukuna i Oketopa 2016, e rima tekau tau i muri i te matenga o Chanie 📖🎶. I haere ia tāra o ngā hua ki ngā mahi pono me te hohou rongo.

I mate a Gord i Oketopa 17, 2017 🕊️, engari koinei te mea: kāore te mahi i mate tahi me ia. Ko te Gord Downie & Chanie Wenjack Fund, i hangaia tahitia e ngā whānau e rua, e rere ana ināianei puta noa i ngā Kura Tuku Iho 9,500 neke atu puta noa i Kanata 🏫🪶, e ako ana ngā tamariki i te ingoa o Chanie me te whakahau kupu-rua a Gord: "Mahia tētahi mea." I tēnei wiki, Oketopa 17 ki te 22, ko te Wiki o Secret Path, e iwa tau i muri i tōna matenga 📅✨. Nō reira ko taku tono tēnei, e te kāhui: akohia tētahi kōrero pono kāore anō koe i whakaakona. Whakahuatia te ingoa o Chanie Wenjack ki te reo nui. Tautokohia te Fund, uru ki tētahi Hīkoi mō Wenjack, whakahaeretia te kōrero uaua ki tō ake tēpu 🗣️🍽️. I whakapau a Gord i tōna tau whakamutunga ki te whakaatu ahakoa he mutunga, ka taea te tīmatanga, ko te mea iti rawa ka taea e au ko te kore e moumou i te mīhini whakakaha reo i tukuna e ia ki a mātou 🎤💛.

He kōwhiringa tonu te anga whakamua, ā, ko te tikanga ko KOE hoki! 💛🚀

🤣😅😄 Te Kata Mahi Whenua o Ia Rā

Ka hīkoi tētahi tangata ki roto i tētahi pāpara me ngā taura whakaoho pēteretere e takai ana i tōna kakī pēnei i te tahei 🚗🔌🍺

Ka neke atu ia ki te pāpara, ā, ka kī: "Ka inumia e au tētahi pia."
Ka tirotiro te kaituku inu i a ia mai i runga ki raro, ka whakaiti i ōna kanohi, ā, ka kī…

"Ka pai, māku koe e mahi, engari kaua e tīmata i tētahi mea!" 😂⚡🍻

🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea E 5 Kāore Ngā Kait mahi Whenua Ngaio e Whāki Ki A Koe 🌿🛠️

1. Whakamātauria te pāmahana o te oneone i mua i te whakatō i te koro kotahi. Ka teka te pāmahana hau ki a koe i Oketopa; ko te pāmahana oneone te pono. Panaia tētahi ine pāmahana kīhini tūpato e whā īnihi ki raro, e hiahia ana koe kia noho tonu te oneone i raro āhua i te 10°C, engari kia kaua anō e hukapapa, i mua i te urunga o ngā tulip me ngā daffodil. Whakatō ki te oneone e mahana tonu ana, ā, ka pihi ngā kōpura i te wā ngāwari, ka tahuna e te hukapapa tūturu tuatahi, ka moumou i te pēteri katoa o te koro. I Edmonton ko taua matapihi reka ko te mutunga o Oketopa i te nuinga o te wā, inea, kaua e whakapae.

2. Horoia te tote mai i ō moenga i mua i te mau a te hukapapa ki roto. Ko ia moenga me ia rīpene orota i mau i te tāwhiwhi tote o te takurua kua taha kei te kawe tonu i te konutai i roto i ngā īnihi torutoru o runga o te oneone. Kotahi te whakaū roa, pōturi, hōhonu i te rā ngahuru mahana, e kōrero ana mātou mō ngā hāora, kaua he tauhiuhi, ka tuku i taua konutai ki raro i te rohe pakiaka i te wā e tuwhera tonu ana te whenua ki te rere. Tatari ki te kōanga, ā, kei te horoi koe i te wā o te waipuke rewa, i te wā e whawhai kē ana te wai rewa ki a koe. Ko te 14 tīkiri o tēnei rā ko te momo rā tonu hei whakahaere i te ngongo.

3. Whakamaua ō pou hukarere ki te tītaha, kaua kia tū tōtika. Ka tūtaki tētahi pou poutū ki te mata parau hukarere tōtika, ā, ka hinga, ka pakaru, ka ripia rānei kia ma, ā, ko te tapa ara i tiakina e ia ka kainga i Pēpuere. Tītahatia te pou āhua 15 tīkiri mai i te ara, e anga whakamuri ana mai i te ahunga e haere ai te parau, ā, ka piki te mata ki runga, ki runga ake, kaua mā roto. He pou kotahi, he meneti e rima kotahi o te mahi, e toru whakareanga te tere ora puta noa i te takurua.

4. Kaua e whāngai haukota ki ngā rākau me ngā mauwha i te ngahuru. He tikanga tō te pupuhi "winterizer" ngahuru mō te pātiotio, ka penapena te tarutaru i roto i ngā pakiaka. He ture kē ō ngā tipu rakau: mā te haukota ināianei e pana ngā tipu hou ngohengohe, ngohe i te wā tonu me whakapakeke te tipu, ā, mā te hukapapa mārō tuatahi e patu taua tipu, e whakawero i te mauwha, te rākau katoa. Tukua ō rākau me ō mauwha kia kati i runga i tā rātou ake wātaka. Mēnā e hiakai ana rātou, whāngaihia i te kōanga i te wā e taea ai e te tipu hou te whakamahi.

5. Pupuritia ngā tapahi ngahuru hei puranga manga, kaua e hari atu. Ko te mahi taunoa ko te tapahi iti, te hari atu, te utu i te utu tuku. Ko te mahi mātātoa ake, i te wā he kokonga tō te kiritaki mōna: tāpukea ngā tapahi ngahuru hei puranga manga tāwari i muri o te iāri. Ka whakamarumaru ngā chickadee me ngā junco i roto puta noa i te takurua, ka pakaru te rakau i tōna wāhi, ā, kātahi koe ka tiaki i te haerenga tuku me te utu i haere tahi mai. He tokomaha ngā kiritaki e aroha ana ki te taha kararehe mohoao ina whakamārama koe, ko te tohutohu onge e tiaki moni ana, e tiaki wā ana, Ā, e tiaki kanohi ana.
