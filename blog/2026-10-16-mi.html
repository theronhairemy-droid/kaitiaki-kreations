🌟 Te Karere o Tēnei Rā: He Tangata Ngā Wāhine: The Famous Five

Me kōrero atu au ki a koe mō ngā wāhine tokorima o Alberta i kore ai e whakaae ki te whakautu i hoatu ki a rātou 🇨🇦💪. Ko Emily Murphy o Edmonton, te kaiwhakawā wahine tuatahi i te Emepaia o Piritana 👩‍⚖️, i aro ia ki te Senate, engari i tūtaki tonu ia ki te pātū kotahi: i raro i te British North America Act, ko ngā "tāngata whai tohu" anake ka taea te whakatū, ā, i tohe te kāwanatanga o taua wā kāore noa ngā wāhine i te tangata i raro i te ture 🚧. Whakaarohia te kōrerotanga atu e tō ake pukapuka ture o tō whenua kāore koe e whai tikanga ā-ture hei tangata 😤. Kāore a Murphy i pōuri mō tēnā, i kimi tangata ia. I whakahuihuia e ia a Henrietta Muir Edwards, a Nellie McClung, a Louise McKinney me Irene Parlby, he kaituhi, he kaihanga ture, he kaiwhakahaere, he mana ia tangata, ia tangata 🌟, ā, i Ākuhata 27, 1927, i pitihana te tokotorima, te tokorima ki Ottawa kia tukuna te pātai ki te Kōti Matua: kei roto i te kupu "tāngata" ngā tāngata wahine? 📜

Ko te wero i tūtaki atu rātou he nui ake i te tūru Senate kotahi 🧱. Ka taea e te nuinga o ngā wāhine o Kanata te pōti i taua wā, ka taea e ētahi te noho i ngā whare ture porowini, tae atu ki te House of Commons, engari i mau tonu te raka o te kuaha Senate, ā, ko te arorau e raka ana i kī ai ko te haurua o te whenua e tū ana i waho o te whakamāramatanga ā-ture o te tangata 🔒. I 1928, i whakautu te Kōti Matua o Kanata i tā rātou pātai me te "kāo" kotahi ❌. E rima wiki o te tautohe, he rautau o te tikanga i whakahuatia, ā, ka pākōkō te kuaha. He tokomaha ngā kaupapa kua mate i reira tonu i ngā arawhata o te whare kōti 💔. I mahi a The Famous Five i te "kāo" hei piko, kaua hei tohu kati, i pīra rātou ki runga ake o te māhunga o te Kōti Matua ki te Komiti Whakawā o te Privy Council i Rānana, te kōti pīra teitei rawa o taua wā i te Emepaia o Piritana ⚖️🌍.

Ko taua whakatau ki te mahi, ki te kawe i te whawhai kotahi kōti teitei ake, kaua ki te hoki ki te kāinga, i whakarerekē i te whenua 🇨🇦✨. I Oketopa 18, 1929, e iwa tekau mā whitu tau ki muri i tēnei Rātapu, i huripokina e te Privy Council te Kōti Matua, ā, i whakatau he "tāngata whai tohu" ngā wāhine, e tika ana mō te Senate 🎉. I kīia e te whakawā ko te whakakore i ngā wāhine mai i ngā mahi tūmatanui he toenga mai i ngā rā nanakia ake i ō mātou, ā, ko tōna tirohanga "rākau ora" ki te Ture Matua kei te hanga tonu i te ture o Kanata i tēnei rā 🌳⚖️. I noho a Cairine Wilson hei senata wahine tuatahi o Kanata i 1930. Te tuhipoka reka kawa: kāore anō a Emily Murphy tonu i whiwhi i te tūru, i hipatia ia, ā, i mate ia i 1933 me te kore noho i te rūma i whakatuwherahia e ia mō ngā tāngata katoa i muri i a ia 🕯️. I hangaia e ia tētahi kuaha kāore anō ia i hīkoi mā roto, ā, i hangaia tonutia e ia.

Kua tuhia te pānga mauroa ki roto i te whenua tonu 💛. Ko Oketopa te Marama Hītori Wāhine i Kanata, ko Oketopa 18 te Rā Tāngata, e tū ana a The Famous Five i te parahi i Parliament Hill, i Calgary hoki, ā, kua whakapaingia ō rātou kanohi i te moni rima tekau tāra 🏛️. Ko ia wahine kua noho i te Senate mai i 1930 i hīkoi mā te whakatuwheratanga i wetekina e aua tāngata tokorima o Alberta me te pitihana me te kore whakaae ki te whakamutu 🔥. Nō reira ko taku karanga mahi tēnei: i tēnei mutunga wiki o te Rā Tāngata, akohia tētahi o ō rātou ingoa kia tika, Murphy, McClung, McKinney, Edwards, Parlby, ā, tukuna te kōrero ki tētahi rangatahi i tō oranga 📣. Kāore ngā motika e rere whakamua i runga i a rātou anō; me pana e tētahi. He kōwhiringa tonu te anga whakamua, ā, ko te tikanga ko KOE hoki!

🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea E 5 Kāore Ngā Kait mahi Whenua Ngaio e Whāki Ki A Koe 🌿🛠️

1. Hikihia, penapenatia ō pakiaka dahlia, canna me gladiolus i muri i te hukapapa tuatahi 🌺
Kāore ngā dahlia, ngā canna me ngā glad e pakari mō Zone 3, waiho ki te whenua o Edmonton, ā, mā te takurua e huri ngā pakiaka ki te paru. Tatari kia whakapango te hukapapa tuatahi i ngā rau (ko taua pānga te tohu ki te tipu kia toia tōna pūngao ki raro), kātahi keria kia āta mā te mārau i waho roa o te puranga, whakamaroketia mō ngā rā torutoru ki tētahi wāhi maroke, hau pai, ā, penapenatia ki te peat, ki te perlite āhua maroke i te 4–7°C. Tapaina ia momo i mua i te penapena, i te kōanga, he ōrite te āhua o ia pakiaka, ā, kei te teka tō mahara ki a koe.

2. Hangaia tētahi pākete onepū me te hinu hei horoi i ō taputapu ringa 🪣
Whakakīia tētahi pākete 5-gallon ki te onepū maroke, ā, whakaranuhia he hinu iti noa (he linseed, he hinu huawhenua ma rānei) kia rite ki te onepū takutai haumākū. Ia wā ka mutu koe me te kō, te kō iti, ngā tapahi rānei, kuhuna te mata ki roto, ki waho, i ngā wā torutoru, ka waru te onepū i te oneone me te wairākau, ā, ko te kiriata hinu angiangi e toe ana ka whakamutu i te waikura kia māeke. Ko ngā taputapu i penapenatia puta noa i te takurua i muri i te haerenga pākete onepū ka puta mai i te kōanga he ma, he āhua koi, kaua he karaka, he mau, ā, e tekau hēkona noa ia taputapu.

3. Ruia te rai takurua ki ngā moenga huawhenua kau ināianei tonu 🌾
Ko te oneone māra kau i Alberta e whakapau ana i te takurua e warua ana e te hau, e patua ana e te hukapapa-rewa, ā, ka kitea e te kōanga he kirikiri, he kore ora. Ko te rai takurua te hua uhi pakari rawa o te katoa, ka whakatupu i te oneone māeke pēnei i te āhua 1°C, nō reira ka mau tonu te rui o waenganui Oketopa, ā, ka pupuri i te moenga me ngā pakiaka ora puta noa i te takurua, ka pehi i ngā taru tōmua, ā, ka kōhuatia i te kōanga hei matū waro kore utu. Pūreretia kia mātotoru, rakauhia kia pāpaku, ā, tukua te wā mahana o tēnei rā ki te mahi i te toenga.

4. Nukuhia te peita latex, te caulk me ngā matū wai ki te rokiroki whakamahana i mua i te pānga o ngā pō māeke 🎨
Ko ngā pō o Rātapu me Mane e pā ana ki te 0°C, ā, ka heke a Tūrei ki te −1°C, ā, mā te hukapapa mārō kotahi e whakangaro puku tētahi taonga o ngā rawa. Ko te peita latex ka hukapapa ka huri ki ngā tupeka rite ki te tīhi kāinga, ā, kāore anō e ora, ka wehe te caulk me ngā whakapiri, ka kiri rānei i roto i te puoto, ā, ka taea e ngā maniua wai me ngā patu ngārara te wāwāhi i ō rātou ipu i te whakawhānuitanga. Tekau meneti i tēnei rā e neke ana i ngā mea katoa ki tētahi wāhi whakamahana ka tiaki i te hoko anō i te kōanga i ngā utu kōanga.

5. Whakawāteatia ngā rau mākū mai i ngā poka matapihi, ā, mai i te taha o te tūāpapa i mua i te hukapapatanga 🍂
Ko te poka matapihi ki tonu i ngā rau mākū he pā, ehara i te whakapaipai, ka mau i te wai rewa ki te taha o te tūāpapa me te matapihi o raro, ā, ina hukapapa taua papatipu mākū ka whakawhānui i te wāhi tonu kāore koe e hiahia pēhanga. He kōrero ōrite mō ngā whāriki rau e tāpuke ana ki ngā pātū tūāpapa me ngā whare arawhata: ka pupuri rātou i te haumākū ki te raima puta noa i ia hurihanga hukapapa-rewa o te takurua. Ko te 18°C maroke o tēnei rā te rā hei raku i a rātou ki waho, tirohia he mārama ngā rere poka, ā, tukuna taua wai atu i te whare, kaua e penapena ki te taha o te pātū.
