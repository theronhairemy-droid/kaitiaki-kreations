🌟 Te Karere o te Rā Nei: Te Tamaiti o Edmonton i Neke ai i te Wā: Michael J. Fox

I whānau mai a Michael J. Fox i te Hune 9, 1961, i konei tonu i Edmonton 🇨🇦, he tamaiti hōia i neke atu i tētahi pā hōia ki tētahi atu i mua i te noho a te whānau ki Burnaby, BC 🌲. I whakarērea e ia te kura kia mahi hei kaiwhakaari, i whiwhi i a Family Ties hei Alex P. Keaton, ā, i te tau 1985 i hurihia e Back to the Future tētahi Kanata 24 tau te pakeke hei whetū kiriata nui rawa atu o te ao 🎬⚡. Mō tētahi tekau tau katoa, kāore rawa te tangata nei i hē, te āhua pai, te wā tika, te kanohi i runga i ia pouaka kai o Amerika ki te Raki 🌎. Mēnā i hiahia koe ki te whakaahua o tētahi oranga kāore he tuanui e kitea ana, ko tāna tērā ✨.

Kātahi, i te tau 1991, i a ia e kiriata ana i Florida, ka kite ia i tōna maihao iti e korikori ana, ā, kāore i mutu 😔. Te tautuhi mate: te mate Parkinson tīmata-tamariki, i te toru tekau tau, i te tihi o ngā mea katoa. I hunaia e ia mō ngā tau e whitu 💔, i mahi tonu, i inu nui rawa, i mataku kei te rā i puta ai te kōrero, ka haere tahi te mahi me te tuakiri. Whakaarohia te kawe i taua mea huna i ia uiui, i ia wāhi kiriata, i ia whakaaturanga tuatahi, e whakaari ana i te māmā hei oranga i te wā e tutū puku ana tōu ake tinana ki a koe 🎭. I te tau 1998 ka kōrero ia ki te ao, i runga i āna ake tikanga, ā, ko tāna mahi i muri mai te take e tika ana tēnei kōrero ki tēnei rangitaki 🔥.

I whakatau ia kia mahi, ā, ehara i te mea iti 🎯. I te tau 2000 i hoki whakamuri ia i Spin City, ā, i whakatū i The Michael J. Fox Foundation me tētahi whāinga he tino māhaki rawa te nuinga o ngā rōpū aroha ki te kōrero nui: kimihia te rongoā, kātahi whakamutua tāna ake mahi 🧪. Ehara i ngā hui whakanui mō rātou anō, engari he pūtea rangahau, e aro ana pēnei i te taputapu, me ngā rā kati e piri ana. I noho ia hei māngai tūroro rongonui rawa atu o te Ao, i kōrero ki mua i te Congress, i tuhi i ngā pukapuka whakamaharatanga hoko-nui i atawhai i tōna mate me te pono me te katakata nui ake i tā te tangata i whakaaro ai 📚, ā, i mahi whakaari tonu i ngā wā i whakaaetia e tōna tinana, nā te mea ehara rawa te mahi i te raruraru. Ko te whakamutu te raruraru 💪.

He tau tō te pānga roa: kua utua e tāna rōpū neke atu i te rua piriona tāra o te rangahau Parkinson, te kaituku pūtea kore-pai nui rawa atu o taua rangahau i te ao 💰🔬, ā, i te tau 2023 i tau mai te pūtaiao i tautokona e ia ki tētahi pakaruhanga i whāia e te mara mō ngā tekau tau: tētahi whakamātautau tohu-koiora ka taea te kite i te mātūtanga o Parkinson i roto i te wai tuara me te tika tata ki te 90%, ahakoa i mua i ngā tohu mate 🧬🎉. I whakawhiwhia ia e Kanata hei Officer of the Order of Canada i te tau 2010, ā, kua whakatairangahia ia ināianei ki Companion, tōna tūranga teitei rawa 🎖️. I whakahōnore te rangitaki o nanahi i a Tommy Douglas i tōna rā whānau, he tangata i huri i tētahi mahi tiaki kore utu hei medicare. Ko tō tēnei rā e whakahōnore ana i tētahi tamaiti whānau-i-Edmonton i huri i tētahi tautuhi mate whakamate hei rua piriona tāra o te tūmanako mō ngā tauhou 🌍. He whawhai rerekē, he whakakāhoretanga kotahi. Nō reira ko tāku karanga mahi tēnei: kei tō porowhita tētahi tangata e kawe puku ana i tētahi tautuhi mate i tēnei wā, ko koe te tangata e pātai ana me pēhea rātou, ā, e pātai tonu ana 📣. He kōwhiringa tonu te anga whakamua, ā, ko KOE hoki tērā!

🛠️🌿 Te Ruku Hōhonu: Ngā Mea e 5 Kāore te Nuinga o ngā Kaiwhakapaipai Whenua Ngaio e Kōrero ki a Koe 🌿🛠️

1. Whakawhitia te rerenga o te papu sump ki te paipa mārō i mua i te Paraire 🚰
Ko taua ngongo rerenga raumati ngāwari he mahanga mātaratara: ka tārere, ā, ka pupuri wai ia wāhi iti. Ka kawea mai e te Paraire tētahi rā ua 90% whai muri ko te mātaratanga kaha, ka mātaratara totoka te wai i roto i te ngongo, ā, i te rewa e whai ake ka pana te papu ki tētahi puru huka, ā, ka hoki kē ki te papa o raro 💧❄️. Whakawhitia ināianei ki te paipa mārō, whānui ake, me te hekenga whakararo tonu atu i te whare, kāore he piko i te rere katoa. Tekau mita o te paipa tōtika, heke, i Oketopa, he pai ake i tētahi papa o raro waipuketia i Pēpuere, i ia wā 🏠.

2. Kaua rawa e parautia te hukarere i runga i te papa septic ❄️🚫
He āhua whakamuri, engari ko te hukarere i ROTO i te papa septic he whakamaru, ehara i te aukati. Paruatia atu (taraiwa, tū hoki ki runga i te papa i te takurua katoa) ā, ka tangohia e koe te paraikete e pupuri pāpaku ana i te haupapa, ka haere hōhonu ake te makariri, ka taea ngā raina me te papa anō te mātaratara, ā, ka whakakikī te taumaha waka i te oneone mātaratara i te wāhi kāore koe e hiahia ana ki te whakakikī 🚗. Waiho te hukarere o te papa ki te wāhi e hinga ana, puritia ia wira ki waho, ā, tukua te paraikete kia mahi i te mahi hangarau. Ko te hukarere hākiki rawa o te rawa e mahi ana i te mahi nui rawa 💪.

3. Kaua e horahia te pungarehu wahie ahi ki ngā moenga o Edmonton 🔥
E kī ana ngā tohutohu o te whenua tawhito mā te pungarehu wahie e whāngai te māra, ā, i runga i te oneone waikawa, ka taea. Ehara te uku oneone o Edmonton i te waikawa: he kawakore kē i te nuinga o te wā, tata ki te pH 7.4–8+ 🧪. He kawakore, he tote hoki te pungarehu, nō reira mā te horahi e piki ake anō te pH, ā, ka tīmata te raka i te rino me te konukura, koirā te take e whiwhi ai koe i ngā tipu kōwhai, kore rino, i te oneone e kī ana he ki tonu i ngā taiora. Ki te kore tētahi whakamātautau oneone e whakaatu pono i te waikawa, nō te para tō pungarehu, nā, ka mutu kino te kōrero o te wairākau. Whakamātautau tuatahi; whakatika tuarua 📋.

4. Tiakina ngā rākau iti i raro i ngā awa tuanui whakarewa kei hinga te hukarere pēnei i te horo whenua 🏠❄️
Kāore te tuanui whakarewa e tuku hukarere i ngā ringa iti māhaki, ka tukua i tētahi pepa reti kotahi, i te wā kotahi, tōtika iho i te raina awa. Ko tētahi rākau iti i whakatōkia i raro i taua raina ka whiwhi i te hukarere katoa o te tuanui i runga i te karauna i te hēkona kotahi: ka whati ngā rangatira, ka pakaru ngā karauna, ā, ko te tipu e kitea e koe i te kōanga he āhua i ngaro tētahi tautohe ki tētahi taraka 🌿💥. Tohua ngā rohe reti ināianei, i te wā e kite mārama tonu ana koe i ngā awa, tiakina ngā mea e pupuritia ana me tētahi anga rakau mārō āhua A, ā, whakamaheretia te neke i ngā mea utu nui katoa ki waho rawa o te raina hinga.

5. Whakarite te rokiroki takurua kia kore e uru te kiore 🐭
Ko tētahi papa mihini tapahi i purua paru he hōtēra kiore me te wera kua tahuna: ko te tarutaru me te oneone kua kikī he rauemi kōhanga tino pai, ā, mā te wharau mahana te toenga, ā, hei te kōanga kei te titiro koe ki ngā waea kua ngaungaua me tētahi kōhanga i te wāhi o te tātari hau 🔧. Hekea, horoia te papa i mua i te urunga. He whakaaro ōrite mō te kākano tarutaru: ko tētahi pēke pepa tuwhera i te karāti he kai tuwhera mō te takurua katoa. Nekehia te kākano ki ngā ipu whakarewa hiri me ngā taupoki kikī, ā, ka taea e ngā kiore te tuku i ā rātou amuamu ki wāhi kē 🗄️.
